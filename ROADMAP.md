# Roadmap: Tertiary SmartLearn LMS vision

This is a forward-looking product vision, not a description of what is built today. It captures ideas for taking this
university LMS well beyond an ordinary LMS or Google Classroom by combining teaching, collaboration, practical
training and intelligent learning support. See `README.md` for what is actually implemented and running today.

Nothing here is scheduled. Treat each numbered area as an independent proposal to be scoped, designed and built on
its own — most of them are substantial projects in their own right (new data models, third-party integrations such
as video conferencing or an AI provider, offline-sync architecture, and so on).

## 1. Personalised smart dashboard

- Different dashboards for students, lecturers, coordinators and administrators
- Daily learning plan
- Upcoming lessons and deadlines
- Missing-work warnings
- Recent lecturer feedback
- Course-progress indicators
- Personal study-time recommendations
- Quick access to weak topics
- Google Calendar synchronisation

## 2. Smart Course Room

Each course has one organised digital space containing:

- Weekly learning units
- Notes, presentations and videos
- Live-class links
- Assignments and quizzes
- Discussion forum
- Practical activities
- Course announcements
- Lecturer consultation times
- Student progress
- Google Drive resources

## 3. Adaptive learning paths

- Identify topics a student is struggling with
- Recommend revision materials
- Generate additional practice questions
- Unlock advanced lessons after prerequisites are completed
- Allow lecturers to assign different work to different students
- Provide weekly progress recommendations
- Create individual catch-up plans

## 4. Low-data and offline learning

- Download complete learning units
- Read notes without internet access
- Complete assignments offline
- Synchronise work when internet returns
- Audio-only option for live lessons
- Compress videos and documents
- Low-data interface
- SMS reminders for important deadlines

This makes the system practical for students with limited or unstable internet access.

## 5. Smart virtual classroom

- Live video and audio lessons
- Google Meet integration
- Built-in low-bandwidth classroom
- Screen and document sharing
- Digital whiteboard
- Live polls
- Student hand-raising
- Breakout discussion groups
- Automatic attendance
- Recorded lessons
- Searchable lesson recordings
- Live questions and lecturer responses

## 6. Advanced assignment centre

- Individual and group assignments
- Draft submission before final submission
- Lecturer feedback on drafts
- Resubmission control
- Late-submission explanations
- Peer review
- Assignment version history
- Voice or video feedback
- Rubric-based marking
- Google Docs collaboration
- Similarity checking

## 7. Smart tests and examinations

- Multiple-choice, essay and practical questions
- Question banks
- Randomised questions
- Different questions for each student
- Automatic and manual marking
- Timed assessments
- Auto-save during examinations
- Internet-disconnection recovery
- Open-book and closed-book settings
- Lecturer moderation
- Examination activity logs
- Detailed performance analysis

## 8. Practical Skills Passport

This differentiates the platform from Google Classroom.

- Record practical competencies
- Digital practical logbook
- Workshop and laboratory activities
- Lecturer observation checklist
- Upload photographic or video evidence
- Supervisor assessment
- Competency status: Not Started, Developing or Competent
- Practical-hours tracking
- Student skills portfolio
- Verified digital skills badge

## 9. Industrial attachment workspace

- Attachment learning objectives
- Weekly student logbook
- Workplace supervisor feedback
- Lecturer visits
- Evidence uploads
- Attendance and working-hours record
- Final attachment report
- Presentation assessment
- Combined supervisor and lecturer mark
- Attachment progress dashboard

## 10. Research and project workspace

- Submit project topics
- Supervisor allocation
- Proposal review
- Meeting records
- Milestone tracking
- Draft chapter submissions
- Supervisor comments
- Similarity reports
- Final project repository
- Presentation scheduling
- Assessment rubrics

## 11. Intelligent gradebook

- Assignment, quiz, test and practical marks
- Weighted assessments
- Continuous-assessment totals
- Lecturer comments
- Grade moderation
- Mark-change history
- Student performance trends
- Early-warning alerts
- Export to PDF, Excel or another institutional system

The LMS should manage learning marks, not official transcripts, graduation or certificates.

## 12. Learning intervention centre

- Detect missing assignments
- Identify declining performance
- Identify inactive students
- Notify lecturers about students needing assistance
- Create intervention plans
- Schedule consultation sessions
- Track whether performance improves
- Send private student support alerts

## 13. Collaborative learning community

- Course discussion forums
- Student study groups
- Peer tutoring
- Shared digital whiteboards
- Group projects
- Knowledge-sharing rooms
- Lecturer question-and-answer sessions
- Academic clubs
- Moderated course communities

## 14. Academic integrity centre

- Plagiarism and similarity reports
- Citation checking
- Submission history
- AI-use declaration
- Assessment activity logs
- Suspicious submission alerts
- Lecturer investigation workspace
- Academic-integrity case records
- Appeal and review process

## 15. AI learning assistant

- Explain course concepts
- Summarise lecturer-provided notes
- Generate revision questions
- Create flashcards
- Produce personal study plans
- Recommend learning resources
- Provide hints without directly completing marked assignments
- Answer questions only from approved course materials
- Show the source material used for each response

## 16. Lecturer teaching assistant

- Generate lesson outlines
- Create draft quizzes
- Build marking rubrics
- Suggest discussion questions
- Identify commonly failed questions
- Summarise class performance
- Recommend remedial lessons
- Reuse and improve course content

## 17. Accessibility and inclusion

- Text-to-speech
- Captions for recorded lessons
- Keyboard navigation
- Screen-reader support
- Adjustable font size and contrast
- Transcripts for audio lessons
- Extended-time assessment settings
- Multiple-language support
- Accessible document checking

## 18. Engagement and motivation

- Learning streaks
- Topic-completion badges
- Skills badges
- Course milestones
- Class participation points
- Progress celebrations
- Personal learning goals

Badges should encourage learning and should not replace official academic results.

## 19. Communication centre

- Course announcements
- Private messages
- Group discussions
- Email and application notifications
- SMS deadline alerts
- Consultation booking
- Emergency class notices
- Notification preferences

> messages" belongs here before building it again.

## 20. Learning analytics

- Student engagement reports
- Course-completion rates
- Difficult-topic analysis
- Assessment-performance trends
- Lecturer response time
- Assignment submission patterns
- Practical competency reports
- At-risk student reports
- Department-level learning reports

## Key features that make it unique

The platform's strongest differentiators, in the original author's view:

1. **Low-data and offline learning**
2. **Practical Skills Passport**
3. **Industrial attachment workspace**
4. **Research and project supervision**
5. **Adaptive learning paths**
6. **Early student-support alerts**
7. **AI restricted to approved course materials**
8. **Internet-disconnection examination recovery**
9. **Voice and video lecturer feedback**
10. **Google Workspace integration without depending entirely on Google Classroom**

A suggested name for this fuller vision: **Tertiary SmartLearn LMS** — a complete learning, practical training and
academic collaboration platform.
